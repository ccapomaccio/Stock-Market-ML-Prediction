Stock Market Prediction & Investment Strategy Analysis

Project Overview
I developed a machine learning project using Python to analyze historical stock market data and predict stock price movements over a five-day period. I analyzed over 27,000 observations across 10 publicly traded companies.

Tools & Technologies
Python | Pandas | Scikit-learn | Matplotlib | Yahoo Finance

Methodology
- Built Logistic Regression and Random Forest models to predict stock price movements.
- Analyzed financial indicators including moving averages, volatility, trading volume, and historical returns.
- Developed and backtested a stock selection strategy against an equal-weight benchmark.

Key Results
- **Logistic Regression Accuracy:** 59.28%
- **Random Forest Accuracy:** 58.95%
- **ML Strategy Average 5-Day Return:** 0.79%
- **Benchmark Average 5-Day Return:** 0.69%

Conclusion
My analysis demonstrated how machine learning can be applied to financial markets to evaluate investment strategies. While the ML strategy produced slightly higher average historical returns, it also carried greater volatility, and the models did not outperform a simple prediction baseline. The backtest was exploratory rather than evidence of a reliable trading advantage.
